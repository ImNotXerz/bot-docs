---
title: "/vouch"
description: "Create, view, browse, and dispute reputation vouches."
---

<Info>
  Vouch commands only work as expected once the vouch channel is configured. See
  [Configuration](/vouch/configuration) for first-time server setup.
</Info>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="/vouch create recipient rating">
    **Who:** Anyone who passes the vouch eligibility checks — not blacklisted, meets the
    server's minimum account/membership age, and isn't on cooldown with this recipient.

    **What it does:** Opens a modal (product/service, amount, transaction reference, review,
    proof URL) → confirmation preview → posts a public vouch card to the configured vouch
    channel.

    **Example:** `/vouch create recipient:@Jane rating:5`
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="/vouch view public_id">
    **Who:** Anyone.

    **What it does:** Ephemeral detail view of a single vouch by its public ID. Moderators
    additionally see risk classification and moderation history.

    **Example:** `/vouch view public_id:VCH-A8F4K2`
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="/vouch history [member] [role]">
    **Who:** Anyone.

    **What it does:** Paginated list of vouches received or given, in this server. `member`
    defaults to yourself; `role` defaults to `received`.

    **Example:** `/vouch history member:@Jane role:given`
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="/vouch dispute public_id">
    **Who:** Only the two people involved in the vouch (voucher or recipient).

    **What it does:** Opens the reason-select → description modal flow to dispute a vouch. Also
    reachable via the **Dispute** button on any public vouch card. A vouch can only have one
    open dispute at a time.

    **Example:** `/vouch dispute public_id:VCH-A8F4K2`
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
