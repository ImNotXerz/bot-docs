---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "Invite Updater, set a delivery channel, and choose which games to track."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Invite the bot">
    Ask whoever manages Updater for your server's invite link. See
    [Installation & Permissions](/updater/installation-permissions) for the
    exact scopes and channel permissions it needs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run /setchannel">
    Run `/setchannel` and pick a channel from the native channel picker, then
    hit **Save**. Updater checks its own permissions in that channel before
    accepting it — if it's missing **View Channel** or **Send Messages**
    there, the panel tells you exactly what's missing instead of failing
    silently later.

    Once it's saved, hit **Send Test** to have Updater post a sample
    notification card into the channel. It isn't a real release — just
    confirmation that delivery works end to end.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose which games to track">
    Nothing is tracked by default. Run `/games` to open the tracking panel,
    pick a category — **Featured**, **Steam**, **Tactical**, **Battle
    Royale**, or **Other** — and use the select menu to turn on the games you
    want, or use **Enable All** / **Disable All** for the whole category.
    Hit **Save Changes** before leaving a category; unsaved selections don't
    stick.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

The first check after you enable a game just establishes a starting point —
it won't post anything for updates that already happened. You'll see your
first notification the next time one of your tracked games actually ships
something new.

## If something is not working

See [Support](/updater/support) for the most common causes — missing channel
permissions, a channel that was never set, or games that were just enabled
and haven't had a fresh release yet.
