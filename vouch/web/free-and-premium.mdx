Three things are free forever, for everyone: **on-site vouching, your profile page, and
leaderboard placement**. That is deliberate rather than a trial — reputation belongs to the
person who earned it, and putting a paywall between someone and their own record would make
the record worth less.

What costs money is the tooling built on top: selling, administering, and white-labelling.

## The split

| Capability | Free | User premium | Server premium |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Public profile at `vouch.foo/@handle` | Yes | Yes | — |
| Receive on-site vouches (synced to Discord) | Yes | Yes | — |
| Give on-site vouches | Yes | Yes | — |
| Leaderboard placement, global and per-server | Yes | Yes | Server board page |
| Trust rating with its full breakdown | Yes | Yes | Server-wide view |
| Browse the marketplace | Yes | Yes | — |
| **Create and manage listings on the web** | No | Yes | — |
| **Server storefront at `/s/your-server`** | No | — | Yes |
| **Analytics** (30 / 90 / 365 days) | No | Yes | Yes, server-wide |
| **Web admin console** (settings, cases, enforcement) | No | — | Yes |
| **Webhooks** | No | Yes | Yes |
| **Priority sync** | No | Yes | Yes |
| **Custom bot** (your name, your avatar) | No | — | Add-on |

<Note>
  User premium and server premium are separate things bought by different people. A seller buys
  user premium for themselves; a server owner buys server premium for their community. Neither
  implies the other.
</Note>

## Redeeming a key

Premium is unlocked with a key rather than a card. A key looks like `VF-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`, and
you redeem it at `vouch.foo/dashboard/redeem`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Head to `vouch.foo` and sign in with Discord. Redeeming is tied to your account, so this
    has to happen first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick what it applies to">
    A **user** key applies to your account and needs nothing else. A **server** key asks which
    server — and you must have Manage Server there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the key">
    The dashboard tells you what was unlocked and when it expires. Redeeming a second key for
    the same thing extends it rather than starting a duplicate.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Spending a server key requires **Manage Server** in that server, checked against Discord at
  the moment you redeem — not against anything we have cached. Being a member is not enough.
</Warning>

## What happens when premium lapses

Nothing is deleted, and nothing you earned is taken away.

Listings you already created stay where they are; you simply cannot create new ones from the
web until you renew. A server that lapses keeps its free page — its leaderboard and its trusted
sellers — and loses the storefront and the console. Vouches, profiles and trust ratings are
untouched in every case, because those are free tier.

The one thing with a grace period is a **custom bot**, which keeps running for 7 days after the
add-on lapses before it stops. A renewal that lands on a Monday morning should not have taken
your server's bot offline over the weekend.

## Priority sync

Premium servers get their cards posted in about a second instead of waiting for the periodic
sweep. It is strictly a latency improvement: on the free tier the card still arrives, just up
to a couple of minutes later. Nothing is ever dropped for being on the free tier.
