---
title: "Custom bot"
description: "Run Vouch. under your own bot's name and avatar, against the same shared ledger."
---

A custom bot is your own Discord application — your name, your avatar, your token — running
the full Vouch. feature set. Your members see your brand on every vouch card, leaderboard and
listing.

It is an **add-on on top of server premium**, not part of it.

<Note>
  The data stays in the shared ledger. Reputation earned through your custom bot still counts
  everywhere on vouch.foo, and your members' profiles still work across every server. You are
  changing the face, not forking the platform.
</Note>

## Setting one up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an application">
    In the [Discord developer portal](https://discord.com/developers/applications), create an
    application and give it the name and avatar you want your members to see.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy its token">
    On the application's **Bot** tab, press **Reset Token** and copy what it shows you. This is
    the only time Discord will display it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it into the console">
    `vouch.foo/admin/your-server` → **Your bot**. We check the token with Discord before storing
    it, so a revoked or mistyped one is refused immediately rather than failing silently later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite it">
    The console gives you an invite link with exactly the permissions the bot needs. Invite it,
    then remove the platform bot if you no longer want both in the server.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your bot is usually connected within half a minute of registering.

## How your token is handled

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="It is encrypted before it is stored">
    The token is encrypted the moment it arrives and only ever decrypted by the process that
    connects your bot to Discord.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It is never shown again">
    No page and no API endpoint returns it. It does not appear in logs, and it is deliberately
    left out of the audit records of your own setup — those get pasted into support tickets.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="If you lose it, rotate">
    Reset the token in the developer portal (which invalidates the old one), then paste the new
    one under **Rotate token**. Your bot reconnects with it automatically, and any error state
    from the old token is cleared.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Removing it deletes it">
    Removing the custom bot is a real delete, not a flag — the point is that we stop holding a
    credential nobody needs. Your server falls back to the platform bot.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Warning>
  Never paste a **user** token. Using a user account as a bot is against Discord's terms and
  gets the account banned — the console refuses one if you try.
</Warning>

## Your bot only works in your server

A custom bot runs the whole Vouch. command tree, and nothing stops you inviting it somewhere
else. If you do, it will politely refuse to do anything there.

That is a deliberate boundary, not a limitation: your bot is tied to your server's data, and a
bot that answered commands in someone else's server would be reading and writing reputation
that does not belong to it.

## Status, and what can go wrong

The console shows what your bot is actually doing, not just what you asked for.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Provisioning** | Registered, waiting to be picked up. Normally seconds. |
| **Running** | Connected and answering commands. |
| **Stopped** | You stopped it, or the add-on lapsed past its grace period. |
| **Error** | It could not connect. The reason is shown — usually a revoked token. |

A bot that crashes is restarted automatically with a backoff. A bot whose token Discord
*refuses* is not retried, because retrying cannot fix it — it is marked with the reason and
waits for you to rotate the token.

<Info>
  One custom bot per server. Two bots answering the same server's interactions would double
  every reply.
</Info>

## When the add-on lapses

Your bot keeps running for **7 days**, then stops. The console shows the countdown, and
redeeming a key clears it immediately.

Stopping the custom bot does not affect your data or your server's configuration — invite the
platform bot back and everything continues where it left off.
